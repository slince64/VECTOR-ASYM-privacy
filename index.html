This is a third-party Garmin watch face with optional KiezelPay paid activation after a trial. KiezelPay and payment service providers process the licensing and checkout information described below. Purchase information is submitted to these providers, not to Garmin; Garmin is not responsible for the handling of such payment information.

1. Overview and data displayed

VECTOR ASYM is a Garmin Connect IQ watch face. It may read date and time, steps, step goals, heart rate, activity calories, distance, floors climbed, battery, weather, sunrise/sunset and solar intensity, plus selected Garmin native complications such as Body Battery, altitude, intensity minutes, recovery time, stress, blood oxygen and pressure as supported by your Garmin device. The watch-face display is drawn locally.

2. Licensing, trial and payment

The app integrates the KiezelPay licensing library (App ID 1073700516). For the 8-hour free trial, paid unlock and subsequent license checks, the library uses Connect IQ background communications and connects to KiezelPay services. A watch activation code, license/transaction status and technical/device identifiers needed for activation may be processed by KiezelPay. The one-time listed unlock price is US$2.99, subject to any checkout-disclosed taxes or processing fees. Payment, receipts, and purchase-email information are handled by KiezelPay and its payment providers under their own terms and privacy policies. The developer does not directly request payment-card details in the watch face.

3. Health and location information

VECTOR ASYM does not intentionally send your heart-rate, steps, floors, calorie or other sensor measurements to the developer or to KiezelPay for payment verification. Weather and sunrise/sunset information may be provided by Garmin services subject to Garmin’s own privacy policy. The watch face requests Garmin’s Positioning permission to read the weather observation location or the watch’s already available last-known position for sunrise/sunset calculations. It does not start continuous GPS tracking, store position history, or send location coordinates to the developer or KiezelPay as part of the watch-face code.

The watch face also requests the Garmin ComplicationSubscriber permission to read only the native watch data selected for its two optional bottom slots. Unavailable data is not fabricated. This information is processed for local on-watch display and is not deliberately transmitted to the developer or to KiezelPay.

4. Storage and sharing

Display preferences and watch-face settings are handled by the Garmin Connect IQ settings system. Licensing and purchase records needed to unlock the app may be retained by KiezelPay or payment processors under their own retention policies. We do not operate an independent analytics server, sell health data, or display third-party advertising.

5. Your choices and rights

You may change settings in Connect IQ, uninstall the watch face, or stop using the service. For a previous purchase, visit https://kzl.io/unlock. For KiezelPay transaction, license, refund, or data-related enquiries, consult https://kiezelpay.com/faq/ or its support team. For developer privacy questions, use “Contact Developer” on the VECTOR ASYM Connect IQ listing. Please do not include sensitive medical details in support messages.

6. Hosting, updates and security

This information page is a static HTML document without embedded analytics, advertising scripts or cookies set by the page itself. Its hosting provider may keep normal web server access logs. We may update this notice when functionality or data handling changes and revise the effective date.
